## Unverifiable / judgment calls (left as written)

- **"Roughly 7 percent of patients with reproducible chest wall tenderness are still having a coronary event"** (`l04`, `c023`, `q012`, quiz extra-check). Lee et al. put chest-wall tenderness in ~6% of confirmed MI; other series report up to 15%. The number is in the sourced range and the teaching point (it shifts probability, it does not exclude) is correct, so it was left alone.
- **HEART "history" scoring in the worked note.** The note's story (exertional, substernal, radiating, diaphoretic, eased by rest) is scored "highly suspicious" (2), which is a deliberately subjective item. Scoring it "moderately suspicious" would give 7 rather than 8. Both sit in the high band; the edit was made only to remove the direct contradiction with scenario `s01`.
- **Alvarado interpretive bands** given as 0–3 / 4–6 / 7–10. The original paper uses 1–4 / 5–6 / 7–8 / 9–10; the three-band simplification is the MDCalc/standard teaching version. Left as written.
- **Ranson mortality "roughly 1% at 0 to 2 points"** — usually quoted as 0–3%. Within range.
- **CSRS "−3 to −2 carries risk near 0.4%"** — MDCalc lists 0.4% at −3; the AAFP category table gives 0.2% for the ≤−2 very-low group. Both are published; left alone.
- **The 2% post-test threshold for "PE ruled out"** and the ~15% PERC ceiling are conventions rather than hard numbers, and are correctly framed as such.
- **`l07` NIHSS "roughly 5 or more is a significant deficit"** — there is no single accepted cut-point; the hedged phrasing is defensible.

## Verdict

Strong batch. The conceptual material (biases, script structure, pretest probability, note and presentation structure, ethics) is accurate throughout, and every 2×2 and NNT calculation in it is arithmetically correct. The errors clustered in exactly one place: the exact criteria and score arithmetic of the named decision rules, where three rules (Ottawa, CURB-65, ABCD2) had criteria that would have produced a wrong score at the bedside, two totals (HEART, Alvarado) contradicted the module's own scenarios, one risk band (CSRS) was overstated by roughly a factor of one band, and one vignette keyed a diagnosis that does not exist at the gestational age given. Thirteen corrections across three files.
